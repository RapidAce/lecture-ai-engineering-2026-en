# LoRA vs QLoRA

- **LoRA**: A PEFT method that freezes pretrained weights, represents weight updates as a low-rank product of two small matrices, and trains only those small matrices. It trains few parameters and saves adapters in small files.
- **QLoRA**: A method that saves memory by quantizing base-model weights (e.g., to 4 bits) and then training with LoRA, allowing smaller GPUs to handle larger models.
